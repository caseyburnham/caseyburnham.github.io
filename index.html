About Casey - Audio Engineer in Denver, Colorado

After moving to Colorado in 2011 — and trading Idaho's potato fields for mountain passes — I earned a degree in Audio Engineering from UCD. For over a decade, I've worked as a live sound and theater engineer. Currently serving as Audio Director at the Arvada Center, I mix musicals, design theatrical sound systems, program show control, and occasionally troubleshoot problems that make you question your career choices.

Outside of work, I spend my time scrambling up peaks, beating up my truck on old mining roads, and taking photos of Colorado's high country. I always drag my dog along, though, he's usually not impressed.
